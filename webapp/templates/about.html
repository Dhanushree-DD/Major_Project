About FetalBrain AI

A deep learning system built to assist in detecting fetal brain abnormalities from ultrasound images with state-of-the-art accuracy.

Our Mission

Early and accurate detection of fetal brain conditions is critical for timely intervention. FetalBrain AI applies a fine-tuned Swin Transformer vision model to ultrasound scans, classifying them into 15 conditions with 98.70% validation accuracy — helping healthcare professionals make faster, more confident decisions.

What We Offer

Powerful, accessible and clinically relevant AI tools

Accurate Detection

98.70% validation accuracy across 15 fetal brain conditions from a single ultrasound scan.

Fast Analysis

Instant classification with confidence scores, straight from the uploaded ultrasound image.

Privacy Focused

Uploads are processed securely and stored per user for review in the prediction history.

The Technology

Powered by a Swin Transformer vision backbone

Swin Transformer

A state-of-the-art vision transformer with a hierarchical feature pyramid and shifted windows, fine-tuned on fetal brain ultrasound data for precise multi-class classification.

15-Class Model

Trained to distinguish 14 abnormality types from normal cases, with per-class confidence scoring returned for every prediction.
